CB-024 Completed

Community-Acquired Pneumonia with Right Middle Lobe Consolidation

Alias: PT-4821 • 52y Male • General Medicine • Ward 4B • Oct 12, 2024

1. Presenting Complaints

Fever, chills, and productive cough with purulent rust-colored sputum for 5 days. Associated right-sided pleuritic chest pain on deep inspiration.

2. History of Presenting Illness

Patient reports sudden onset of high-grade fevers and rigors 5 days prior to presentation. Developed productive cough with copious yellowish-rust sputum on Day 2. Reports localized sharp right lateral chest pain exacerbated by coughing. Denies dyspnea at rest, hemoptysis, calf swelling, or recent hospitalization.

3. Clinical & Past History

Past Medical: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus diagnosed 6 years ago on Metformin 500mg BID. No history of asthma or COPD.

Personal & Social: 15 pack-year smoking history, quit 2 years ago. Moderate alcohol intake.

Family History: Father had ischemic heart disease; mother alive and well.

4. General & Systemic Examination

Vitals: BP 128/78 mmHg, HR 96 bpm regular, RR 22/min, Temp 38.6 °C, SpO2 94% on room air.

Respiratory: Decreased chest expansion on right hemithorax. Dullness to percussion over right middle lobe. Bronchial breath sounds and inspiratory coarse crackles localized to right anterior chest.

5. Investigations & Important Findings

Laboratory: WBC: 14.8 × 109/L (86% neutrophils). CRP: 94 mg/L.

Chest X-Ray (PA view): Dense alveolar consolidation in the right middle lobe with visible air bronchograms. No pleural effusion.

Microbiology: Gram-positive diplococci on sputum culture (Streptococcus pneumoniae pending sensitivities).

6. Differential Diagnoses

  1. Community-Acquired Pneumonia (S. pneumoniae) — most likely clinical and radiological presentation.
  2. Acute Pulmonary Embolism with Infarction — considered due to localized pleuritic pain, but low clinical probability on Wells scoring.
  3. Aspiration Pneumonia — less likely; absence of dysphagia, sedation, or impaired consciousness.
verified
Confirmed Clinical Diagnosis

Community-Acquired Pneumonia (Streptococcus pneumoniae, severe outpatient / ward level, CURB-65 score 1)

8. Treatment, Outcome & Follow-up

Initial Regimen: IV Ceftriaxone 2g daily + Oral Azithromycin 500mg daily. Supplemental oxygen 2L/min via nasal cannula.

Clinical Course: Defervescence achieved within 48 hours. SpO2 normalized to 98% on ambient air. Transitioned to oral Amoxicillin-Clavulanate on Day 4.

Follow-up: Scheduled outpatient review with repeat CXR in 6 weeks to confirm radiological resolution.
